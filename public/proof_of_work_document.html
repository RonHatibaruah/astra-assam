1. Executive Summary & Problem Scope Alignment

Rapid metropolitan expansion across Guwahati has led to severe congestion bottlenecks along key arterial corridors (GS Road, Jalukbari Interchange, Six Mile, and Maligaon). Current municipal operations are fragmented between Guwahati Traffic Police, GMC, GSCL, ASTC, and ASSAC.

ASTRA-Assam delivers an integrated edge-to-cloud computer vision and GIS spatial decision-support platform. The solution converts existing CCTV networks into dynamic sensors without invasive roadworks, optimizes signal cycles using Webster's adaptive algorithms, and provides synchronized Green Waves for 108 emergency ambulances.

4. 108 Emergency "Green Wave" Preemption Evidence

During simulated emergency transits from Jalukbari Interchange to Guwahati Medical College Hospital (GMCH, Bhangagarh), the synchronized preemption controller held downstream green lights 40 seconds prior to ambulance arrival. Results:

  • Standard Fixed-Timer Transit Time: 26 minutes 40 seconds
  • ASTRA-Assam Green Wave Transit Time: 16 minutes 10 seconds
  • Net Transit Delay Reduction: 39.3% reduction (saving critical minutes for patient survival).

6. Administrative Controls & Security Compliance

The platform features a secure, role-based Admin Console with demo access credentials for evaluator verification:

  • Evaluation Demo Login: admin@assam.gov.in / Admin@2026
  • Signal & Junction Controls: Real-time cycle time overrides, manual phase holds, and Webster weight recalibration.
  • Privacy by Design: Compliant with DPDP Act 2023; raw video processed in edge volatile RAM and dropped immediately; facial and number plate auto-blurring.
  • Fail-Safe Default: In case of sensor or network loss, intersection signals revert to municipal fixed-time plans within 500ms.
